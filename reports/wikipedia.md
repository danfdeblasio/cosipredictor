# Finding missing Wikipedia CompBio articles

Wikipedia fetched 2026-11-20. COSI model: `model-16.joblib`. Python 3.12.3, scikit-learn 1.8.0, pandas 2.2.1, NumPy 1.26.4.

Generated by `experiments/wikipedia/report.py` from `cache/wikipedia/results.json`, `cache/wikipedia/hideseek.json` and `experiments/wikipedia/review/`. Rerun the scripts in `experiments/wikipedia/` to rebuild it.

## Caveats

- One annotator labelled the review sheet; a second annotator is recommended.
- Labels for articles 21–30 were first drafted with another AI assistant and then checked by the annotator.
- Labels for articles 44, 46, 62, 68, 79, 96 and 98 were set by the agent from the annotator's description, under the agreed rules.
- The hide-and-seek pool counts every candidate outside the collection as not CompBio; some are, so the metrics are a lower bound.
- `list=random` cannot be seeded: the random controls are the titles stored in `sets.json`.
